Founders Background & Profile

Rajendran Sundaram

Bachelor of Laws (LL.B Hons) • Registered Property Manager PM (3) 0162

Rajendran Sundaram, the founder and Principal of Pioneer Property Management, established his professional career in institutional financial governance. Starting as a commercial banking officer with MBf Finance Bhd followed by AmBank Bhd as Head of Recovery, he directed distressed debt work-outs, asset liquidations, and financial audits before transitioning to real estate and property operations in 2005.

He is a graduate holding a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) from the University of Wolverhampton, United Kingdom. As a formally registered Property Manager with the Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers (LPPEH), his legal acumen gives Pioneer an unmatched competence in interpreting and executing the Strata Management Act 2013 (Act 757) and its corresponding statutory subsidiary regulations.

With over 19 years of hands-on experience governing high-density multi-tiered strata schemes, commercial business towers, and industrial estates, Rajendran ensures every Joint Management Body (JMB) and Management Corporation (MC) under his care operates with audited statutory solvency and zero compliance liabilities.
