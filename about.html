Institutional Stewardship Built on Legal Rigor

Founded on the principle that property management is fundamentally a fiduciary trust, Pioneer Property Management (Registered Firm PM (3) 0162) operates under direct oversight of the Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers (LPPEH). With corporate entity registration SSM CA0356984-X, our firm provides licensed assurance to Joint Management Bodies (JMB), Management Corporations (MC), and institutional asset owners across Malaysia.

We bridge physical maintenance with statutory rigor. Every building audit, sinking fund forecast, AGM conduct, and preventive maintenance cycle is carried out according to the Malaysian Property Management Standards (MPMS) and the Strata Management Act 2013 (Act 757), safeguarding long-term asset valuations and resident security.

Act 757 Operational Protocols

How We Manage Your Strata Asset

Every property under Pioneer's management adheres to strict, transparent operational milestones.

01

Initial Building Handover & Diagnostic Audit

Comprehensive baseline review of all M&E systems, lift certifications, fire protection systems, architectural defects, and historical maintenance accounts.

02

Transparent Sinking Fund Projection (10-Yr Plan)

Long-term capital budgeting for lift overhauls, exterior facade repainting, waterproofing, and water pump replacement to avert emergency levy assessments.

03

Defaulter Recovery & Form 11 Enforcement

Systematic notice issuance according to statutory schedules under Act 757, leading to high collection rates while preserving community harmony.

04

AGM & EGM Statutory Execution

End-to-end administration of mandatory General Meetings, proxy verification, motion management, and statutory filings with the Commissioner of Buildings (COB).
