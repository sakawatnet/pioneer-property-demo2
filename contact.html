Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Strata Advisory & Takeover Protocol

Straightforward answers to the primary operational concerns facing Joint Management Bodies, Management Corporations, and Commercial Asset Trustees.

timelapse

What is your proposal response turnaround?

Initial document and parameter verification is conducted within 4 business hours. Following an on-site physical baseline inspection of your facilities, a full formal management tender proposal is rendered within 3 to 5 business days.

checklist_rtl

How is the site audit procedure conducted?

Our qualified building surveyors and certified facility managers conduct a comprehensive audit inspecting mechanical plants (pumps, generator sets, lift machinery), fire safety adherence, security zoning, and structural waterproofing to establish an unbiased asset health baseline.

published_with_changes

What is the takeover transition timeline?

Standard transitions span 30 to 60 days in compliance with Strata Management Act 2013 protocols. We oversee complete documentation retrieval, ledger migration, service contractor vetting, and financial trust account segregation without disruption to daily security or cleanliness.

security

How are client maintenance funds protected?

In compliance with LPPEH mandates, client funds are held strictly within segregated, designated Client Trust Accounts under the building’s name. Pioneer Property Management enforces dual-authorization controls with Committee office-bearers, precluding unvetted capital disbursement.
